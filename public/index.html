What is ShiftScribe?

A simple tool for support workers. It turns your spoken or typed notes into NDIS-style records, so you spend less time on paperwork and more time with your clients.

You say or type

Hi, it’s Sam. I was with Jordan from 9 till 3. We went to the library and practised budgeting. No medications today, and he was in a great mood.

You get a shift note

ParticipantJordan
WorkerSam
Time9:00 am to 3:00 pm
ActivitiesLibrary visit, budgeting practice
MedicationsNone

Don’t worry about getting it perfect

It doesn’t matter if you stutter, say um, change your mind or lose your train of thought. The AI only picks out the important parts.

Um, so, uh, we went to the library and, like, sorry, yeah, did some budgeting. Oh wait, um, no medications today.

Highlighted kept in your recordFaded left out

How to use it

Four quick steps. It takes about a minute.

1

Pick a shift note, incident report, behaviour record or handover.

2

Press start and speak. Stuck? Tap What to say.

3

Tap Generate record and your notes are formatted in seconds.

4

Check

Fill in anything highlighted, then copy or download Word or PDF.

Colour guide

After you generate a record, each box is coloured so you know what to do next.

Red: requiredMissing and needed. Please fill it in.
Yellow: not mentionedYou didn’t say it, so it shows N/A. Change it if needed.
Blue: please checkThe AI wasn’t sure. Make sure it’s right.
